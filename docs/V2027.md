## Formulas and Rounding

Let `f = split ? 2 : 1`, `x = floor(zvE / f)`,
`y = (x - basicAllowance) / 10000`, and `z = (x - 17799) / 10000`.
Each upper boundary is inclusive:

| Zone | Integer income range        | Unrounded tariff tax           | Analytic marginal rate            |
| ---- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| 1    | 0 through basic allowance   | 0                              | 0                                 |
| 2    | allowance + 1 through 17799 | `(q1 * y + 1400) * y`          | `(2 * q1 * y + 1400) / 10000`     |
| 3    | 17800 through 70600         | `(170.74 * z + 2397) * z + c2` | `(2 * 170.74 * z + 2397) / 10000` |
| 4    | 70601 through 249999        | `0.42 * x - s1`                | 0.42                              |
| 5    | 250000 through 279999       | `0.45 * x - s2`                | 0.45                              |
| 6    | 280000 and above            | `0.47 * x - s3`                | 0.47                              |

Use annual `q1`, `c2`, `s1`, `s2`, and `s3` from the table. In 2027 zone 2 starts at 12565; in 2028 it starts at 12901. Do not derive offsets from preceding-zone accumulation or force continuity: extrapolating adjacent linear formulas to 250000 and 280000 gives differences of EUR 0.03 and EUR 0.02 respectively for both years.

Income tax is `floor(tariffTax) * f`, not `floor(tariffTax * f)`.
Solidarity is the lower of `incomeTax * 0.055` and
`(incomeTax - 20350 * f) * 0.119`, or zero at/below the exemption;
fractions of a cent are discarded after choosing the lower candidate.
Marginal income rates are truncated to four decimal places. Average income and
solidarity rates divide by the original unrounded zvE and use four-place half-up
rounding; the displayed total rate adds these rounded components. Zero income
returns zero amounts and rates.

## API and Validation

`deIncomeTax(income, yearOrParameters, options?)` dispatches by built-in year or
the discriminant `version`. `deIncomeTaxV2027` and
`deSupportedTaxParametersV2027` are exported from `.` and `./de`, alongside
versioned parameter, annual formula, option, and supported-year types. There are
no new package subpaths, network calls in production, or changes to result shape.

V2027 validates complete objects, increasing non-negative safe-integer boundaries,
finite non-negative coefficients/subtrahends/exemptions, rates in [0, 1], finite
non-negative taxable income no greater than `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER`, and
boolean non-nullish splitting. Nullish JavaScript splitting retains the existing
single-assessment default. Custom tariffs are accepted without requiring official
coefficients or continuity; they are not assertions of legal validity.

## Test Evidence and Limitations

- `tests/de/draft/V2027-vectors.ts` transcribes draft coefficients as integer hundredths and independently expands the polynomials with BigInt rational arithmetic. Its separate draft vectors share only the case-selection matrix, not production parameter tables, evaluators, or Decimal arithmetic.
- `tests/de/V2027-fixtures.spec.ts` is the complete V2027 offline suite. Its explicitly named statutory draft groups check both annual sets, every boundary with offsets -4 through +4 euros, fractional and odd joint incomes, all six zones and midpoints, analytic derivatives, discontinuities, solidarity onset/mitigation/crossover, rounding, high incomes, validation, custom parameters, immutability, and public declarations/imports. Future approved official BMF comparisons extend this same suite without replacing the independent draft tests.
- All 902 official 2021-2026 JSON and compressed HTML records remain unchanged and are checked through the shared public-entry-point assertions. Draft vectors are not official BMF fixtures.
- `V2021-fixtures.spec.ts` and `V2021-live.spec.ts` register only 2021-2026. The dedicated `V2027-fixtures.spec.ts` and `V2027-live.spec.ts` register only 2027-2028, reusing infrastructure under `tests/de/bmf/`. Official V2027 fixtures activate from approval metadata; live comparisons activate independently from successfully discovered form availability.
- Live availability is successfully parsed from the BMF form, not inferred from implemented years or stored fixtures. Every offered implemented year gets the full matrix. Only genuinely unavailable years skip with diagnostics; outages, parser failures, numerical mismatches, and disappearing active years fail. Offline mocks validate this policy.
- The existing scheduled/manual live workflow remains separate from offline PR CI. Explicit fixture capture for 2027/2028 fails before writes if unavailable; default capture selects only approved stored years.

Draft-formula agreement is not proof of final legislation, official certification,
or exhaustive correctness for every input. Payroll, deductions, child benefits,
tax-return filing, and gross-to-net calculations remain out of scope.
